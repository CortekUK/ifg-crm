# DESIGN.md

## Design Goal
The IFG website should feel like a premium international football education institution. It should combine elite sports energy, academic credibility, and a high-converting user journey.

The target feeling is:
- Nike Academy
- elite football pathway
- professional club recruitment page
- international football education institution
- modern sports editorial website

It should not feel like:
- a SaaS landing page
- an AI startup website
- a CRM login page
- a generic template
- a grassroots local club site
- a dark dashboard

## Visual Direction
The website should feel physical, real, athletic, credible, and premium.

Use:
- strong football imagery
- real player/facility/programme photos where available
- bold uppercase sports typography
- deep black/navy backgrounds
- clean white/off-white content sections
- controlled red accents
- strong editorial image blocks
- partner logos and credibility markers
- large programme tiles
- clear CTA areas

Avoid:
- abstract gradient blobs as the main visual identity
- generic stock icons
- tiny SaaS-style feature cards
- overuse of blue tech CTAs
- generic mockups
- overly clean empty sections with no football energy
- vague cards that could belong to any business

## Colour Direction
Primary colours:
- near-black
- deep navy
- white
- off-white/light grey

Accent colours:
- controlled red for sports energy and important highlights
- blue only if already part of UI states or CRM consistency, not as the main brand feeling

Avoid making the website look like a blue SaaS product.

## Typography
Use bold, condensed, uppercase headings for a sports/institutional feel.
Use clean readable body text for parents and players.

Rules:
- Headings should feel confident and editorial.
- Body text should be legible and not too small.
- Avoid excessive all-caps for long paragraphs.
- Maintain strong hierarchy between section label, heading, supporting copy, and CTA.

## Layout Principles
The layout should feel designed, not assembled.

Use:
- large visual hero areas
- strong image-led sections
- editorial two-column layouts
- premium programme cards
- structured comparison sections
- clear section rhythm
- balanced dark and light sections
- strong spacing
- mobile-first stacking

Avoid:
- endless small cards
- bland white sections
- repetitive equal grids
- content that feels like a dashboard
- sections that look like generic feature blocks

## Hero Section Rules
The hero should immediately communicate:
- football
- education
- pathway
- credibility
- aspiration

The hero should support real IFG imagery or video stills. It should not rely purely on abstract backgrounds.

Hero should include:
- strong headline
- clear subheading
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA
- partner/credibility indicators where appropriate
- optional subtle info card for age range/pathway types

## Programme Card Rules
Programme cards are a key decision point and must feel premium.

Each programme card should include:
- large relevant image
- programme name
- programme type/category
- location
- duration or timing where known
- short benefit-led summary
- clear CTA

Programme cards should not feel like SaaS feature cards.

Avoid unrelated imagery. If real assets are unavailable, use placeholders clearly intended for real football imagery.

## Partner and Credibility Rules
Partners and institutions should feel important.

Do not reduce major credibility points to tiny grey tags.

Use:
- larger logo placeholders/cards
- supporting credibility copy
- partner/institution names
- relationship type where known

Be careful with claims. Do not imply official endorsement or partnership unless the source content confirms it.

## Social Proof and Media Rules
The current IFG site has valuable media/social proof energy. The new version should include a modern version of this.

Use sections such as:
- Inside the IFG Pathway
- Real Players. Real Pathways.
- Player Stories
- Programme Outcomes

The section should support:
- player photos
- video thumbnails
- social-style image grid
- player names
- programme labels
- outcomes/status

Avoid anonymous generic testimonials where possible.

## CTA Section Rules
CTA sections should feel emotional, reassuring, and premium.

Good CTA copy themes:
- Start your application
- Find the right pathway
- Speak with the IFG team
- Not sure which programme fits you?

Avoid generic SaaS phrases like:
- Get started today
- Unlock your potential
- Transform your future

## Form Design Rules
Forms should be clean, trustworthy, and not overwhelming.

The enquiry form should clearly capture:
- full name
- email
- phone
- programme of interest
- player age
- country
- message

Optional useful fields:
- parent/player role
- preferred start date
- current football level

Do not make the form too long without good reason.

## Mobile Rules
Mobile must feel polished, not just stacked.

Check:
- hero readability
- CTA visibility
- programme card scanning
- form usability
- nav clarity
- spacing between sections
- image crop quality

## Final Design Standard
A client should open the preview and feel:
"This keeps the strength of IFG, but it is sharper, more premium, and easier to convert from."
