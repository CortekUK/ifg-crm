# CONTENT_GUIDE.md

## Tone
Premium, confident, direct, credible, and parent-friendly.

The copy should feel aspirational but grounded. IFG should sound like a serious football education pathway, not a motivational poster.

## Audience Awareness
Write for both:
- ambitious players who want opportunity and development
- parents who care about safety, education, credibility, structure, and realistic outcomes

## Copy Principles
1. Be clear before being clever.
2. Explain programme differences simply.
3. Avoid overpromising professional football outcomes.
4. Make the next step obvious.
5. Use proof and specifics where available.
6. Keep copy concise but meaningful.
7. Avoid vague claims without evidence.

## Preferred Language
Use phrases like:
- football development
- academic pathway
- player environment
- structured progression
- international experience
- professional standards
- education and football together
- guided application
- programme pathway
- player support
- competitive match experience
- training environment
- progression route

## Avoid Generic Phrases
Avoid:
- unlock your potential
- take your game to the next level
- chase your dreams
- world-class opportunities everywhere
- game-changing experience
- future stars
- elite in every sentence
- transform your life
- join the revolution

## Positioning Copy
Preferred positioning:
"IFG helps ambitious players combine football development, education, and international pathway opportunities through structured programmes built around players and their families."

Alternative:
"A football and education pathway for players who want to develop in a serious environment while continuing their academic progression."

## Homepage Copy Direction
The homepage should quickly answer:
- What is IFG?
- Who is it for?
- What programmes are available?
- Why should we trust them?
- What happens after applying?
- Which pathway is right for me?

## Programme Page Copy Direction
Each programme page should answer:
- Who is this programme for?
- Where is it based?
- How long is it?
- What does it include?
- What level is required?
- Is education included?
- Is accommodation included?
- What are the outcomes?
- How do I apply?

## CTA Copy
Primary CTAs:
- Start Your Application
- Apply for a Programme
- Apply Now

Secondary CTAs:
- Explore Programmes
- Find Your Pathway
- Speak to the Team
- View Brochure

Supportive CTA copy:
"Not sure which programme fits you? Speak with the IFG team and we’ll help guide you toward the right pathway."

## FAQ Topics
FAQs should cover:
- programme differences
- football level required
- age ranges
- accommodation
- education
- international students
- visas/travel where relevant
- costs/payment where available
- application process
- what happens after applying

## Claims and Accuracy
Do not make unsupported claims.

Be careful with:
- guaranteed outcomes
- professional contracts
- official partnerships
- scholarship claims
- university admissions
- visa guidance
- employment outcomes

If details are uncertain, use neutral language or placeholders requiring client confirmation.

## Style Rules
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use benefit-led headings.
- Use simple, confident sentences.
- Avoid overusing commas.
- Avoid filler adjectives.
- Prefer specifics over hype.
